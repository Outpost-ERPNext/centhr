# Apply for an IOU

Employees apply for an IOU through the IOU application form at `/iou-application`. They do not need a CentHR login.

## Before you apply

You can have only one IOU at a time. You cannot apply when:

- Another IOU application of yours waits for approval.
- A paid IOU of yours is not fully settled.

## Apply

1. Open `/iou-application` on the HR site.
2. Select the company and enter your employee ID.
3. Select **Send Code** and enter the code from your company email. See [Self-Service Forms](../attendance-and-leave/self-service-forms).
4. Enter the IOU amount.
5. Select the **Purpose Type**: Travel or Other.
6. For Travel, enter the **Tenure (Days)**: the number of days of the trip.
7. Enter the purpose of the IOU.
8. Enter your bank name and account number.
9. Select the declaration. It says that any amount outstanding after the due date is deducted from your salary.
10. Select **Submit**.

The form shows your outstanding IOU amount, if you have one.

## What happens next

Your IOU goes to your line manager (HOD), then Finance 1, then Finance 2. You get an email at each step. When Finance 2 pays, the settlement period starts. See [Due Dates and Salary Recovery](due-dates-and-recovery).

## Messages you can see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Select the Purpose Type of the IOU." | Select Travel or Other. |
| "Enter the travel tenure as a number of days." | Enter the tenure for a travel IOU. |
| "Tick the declaration to apply for the IOU." | Select the declaration. |
| "Your IOU application … is still pending approval." | Wait until it is approved or rejected. |
| "You have not settled IOU …" | Submit an IOU claim for the open IOU first. |
