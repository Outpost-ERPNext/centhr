# Claim Expenses

An IOU claim shows how you spent an IOU. It settles the IOU. You can also claim expenses that you paid yourself, without an IOU.

Employees submit claims through the IOU claim form at `/iou-claim`.

## Submit a claim

1. Open `/iou-claim` on the HR site.
2. Select the company and enter your employee ID.
3. Select **Send Code** and enter the code from your company email.
4. If you have no IOU and paid the expenses yourself, select **Claim Expense from Pocket**. Otherwise, the form links your open IOU.
5. Add one row for each expense: the claim type, the description and the amount.
6. Attach the invoice or receipt, stamped by Finance, on each row.
7. Select **Submit**.

## How the claim settles the IOU

| Claim amount | Result |
|---|---|
| Equal to the IOU outstanding | The claim settles the IOU. The IOU is closed. |
| Less than the IOU outstanding | The outstanding amount goes down by the claim. Settle the rest with another claim, or it is deducted from your salary after the due date. |
| More than the IOU outstanding | The claim settles the IOU. Finance pays you the difference. |
| From pocket | Finance pays you the full claim. |

## Rules

- You can file one claim at a time against an IOU. File the next claim after the last one is approved or rejected.
- You cannot file a claim against an IOU after its due date. The outstanding amount is deducted from your salary.

## Approval

A claim goes to your line manager (HOD), then to Finance 1. The approval of Finance 1 is the last step. If Finance must pay you an amount, Finance 1 pays it after the approval.

## Messages you can see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Row …: attach the invoice or receipt stamped by Finance." | Attach the receipt on that row. |
| "You have no open IOU to settle. Tick 'Claim Expense from Pocket' …" | Select Claim Expense from Pocket. |
| "IOU … was due on …. Claims can no longer be filed against it …" | Contact Finance. The amount is deducted from salary. |
| "Your IOU claim … against IOU … is still pending approval." | Wait for the decision on the last claim. |
