# Leave Policy

CentHR enforces the group leave policy on every leave application. The rules do not depend on the person who approves the leave.

## Rules by leave type

| Leave type | Days | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual Leave** | By grade (see below) | Earned each month. Confirmed employees only. Apply in advance. Up to 5 days carry forward and expire on 31 March. |
| **Casual Leave** | 5 a year | Confirmed employees only. Apply in advance. Only after Annual Leave is used up. |
| **Sick Leave** | No practical limit | Attach a medical certificate for more than 3 days. |
| **Maternity Leave** | 112 | Confirmed female employees. Apply in advance. Can be combined only with Annual Leave applied before it. Includes holidays. |
| **Paternity Leave** | 14 | Confirmed male employees. Apply in advance. Must end within 30 days of the birth. Includes holidays. |
| **Adoption Leave** | 60 | Confirmed female employees. Child of 6 months or younger. Apply 30 days in advance. Includes holidays. |
| **Compassionate Leave** | 5 a year | — |

"Confirmed" means that the **Final Confirmation Date** on the employee record is on or before the leave date.

"Apply in advance" means the leave cannot start before the date of the application.

## Annual Leave by grade

| Grade | Days a year |
|---|---|
| Junior Level | 15 |
| Medium Level | 20 |
| Senior Level | 25 |
| Expat | 30 |

These are the default values. HR can change the **Annual Leave Days** on each grade in [Org Master](../employee-master/org-master).

## When leave is credited

A daily job credits leave:

- Yearly leave types are credited on 1 January, or on the date of joining.
- Annual Leave is added each month, by grade and days worked.
- Employees get their leave when they are confirmed.

If the job did not run on a day, it catches up on the next run.

## Messages you can see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "*Leave type* is only for confirmed employees." | Check the Final Confirmation Date of the employee. |
| "*Leave type* must be applied in advance; it cannot be backdated." | Change the from date to today or later. |
| "*Leave type* can only be taken once Annual Leave is used up." | Apply for Annual Leave first. |
| "Enter the child's date of birth for *leave type*." | Enter the date of birth of the child. |
| "Attach a certificate from an authorised medical officer for Sick Leave longer than 3 days." | Attach the medical certificate. |
| "*Leave type* cannot be combined with *other leave*." | Change the dates so the leaves do not touch. |
