# Employee Master

The Employee Master holds the people and the structure that every other module uses: employees, their leave entitlement, their shifts, their holidays and the organization setup.

| Page | Read it to |
|---|---|
| [Employees](employees) | Create, change and remove employee records |
| [Cross-Company Reporting](cross-company-reporting) | Let an employee report to a manager in a different company |
| [Leave Allocations](leave-allocations) | Give employees their leave entitlement |
| [Shifts](shifts) | Define working hours and assign shifts |
| [Holiday Lists](holiday-lists) | Set public holidays and weekly days off |
| [Org Master](org-master) | Maintain departments, grades, designations and other setup records |

Set up the Org Master records and the holiday list of a company before you create its employees.
